## Where we work

### The Early Cancer Institute

The Crispin Lab is based at the Early Cancer Institute, part of the University of Cambridge's Department of Oncology. The Institute brings together researchers focused on detecting cancer earlier and intervening sooner — from risk prediction and early detection to understanding how cancers start.

{{< photo src="/images/environment/early-cancer-institute.jpg" caption="The Early Cancer Institute" >}}

### Cambridge Biomedical Campus

The Institute sits on the Cambridge Biomedical Campus, one of the largest centres of health science in Europe. The campus is home to Addenbrooke's and Royal Papworth hospitals, the CRUK Cambridge Institute, the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology and many academic and industry partners — so the clinicians who collect our data, and the teams who generate it, are a short walk away.

{{< photo src="/images/environment/campus.jpg" caption="Cambridge Biomedical Campus" >}}

### Clinical partners & networks

We work closely with oncologists, radiologists and pathologists at Cambridge University Hospitals, and are part of the CRUK Cambridge Centre (where Mireia co-leads the Ovarian Programme) and the Mark Foundation Institute for Integrated Cancer Medicine. We collaborate with the Department of Radiology and with national and international consortia such as IMPACT-OC.

### The University & colleges

The University of Cambridge is a collegiate university: alongside their department, many lab members belong to one of its colleges, which offer a second, interdisciplinary community — dining, societies, sport and support.

{{< photo src="/images/environment/cambridge.jpg" caption="Cambridge" >}}

### Getting here

The Cambridge Biomedical Campus is a short bus or cycle ride from Cambridge city centre and Cambridge station, with fast, direct trains to London.

## How we work

### Our values

We agreed these values together at our 2026 lab away day:

- **We are transparent and trustworthy**, in both our research and our communication.
- **We are a supportive community**, fostering collaboration within the lab and beyond it.
- **We use innovative techniques to answer questions that matter.**
