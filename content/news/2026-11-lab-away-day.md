This November we left our desks for our annual lab away day: a chance to welcome our newest members, step back from day-to-day projects and think together about where we are heading as a group.

{{< photo src="/images/gallery/g47.jpg" caption="The Crispin Lab at our 2026 away day" >}}

## Breaking the ice

We started with an icebreaker run by [Emma](/people/emma-cook/) and [Ollie](/people/ollie-jansen/): a game of Jenga with a question written on every block. Each block pulled out meant answering its question, from *"What is your favourite quote?"* to *"If you won the lottery, what would you do?"*. It was a fun way for new and longer-standing lab members to get to know each other, and the tower held out longer than anyone expected.

## The future of the Crispin Lab

Next, [Hannah](/people/hannah-clayton/) and [Rebecca](/people/rebecca-wray/) led a workshop on the future of the lab. We talked about where our field is going and how AI fits into it, both as the focus of our research and as a tool in our everyday work. We looked at the skills we will need to develop, how we can build them, and how we stay competitive as a group in a field that is changing quickly.

{{< photo src="/images/gallery/g31.jpg" caption="Hannah and Rebecca leading the \"Future of the Crispin Lab\" workshop" >}}

## Our values

[Hania](/people/hania-paverd/) then hosted a workshop on our values as a lab. We started with what we already value about how we work: having lunch together, the snack desk that keeps coffee breaks going, regular group meetings, and postdocs who support our PhD students. We then discussed what we would like to change, including more "future-proofing" sessions to build the skills we need to keep our work rigorous and trustworthy.

From this discussion we agreed three values for the lab:

- **We are transparent and trustworthy, in both our research and our communication.**
- **We are a supportive community, fostering collaboration within the lab and beyond it.**
- **We use innovative techniques to answer questions that matter.**

{{< photo src="/images/gallery/g34.jpg" caption="Hania leading our lab values workshop" >}}

## Curry and wreaths

With the hard work done, we shared an Indian takeaway before a short walk (or drive!) to a wreath-making workshop. There we each designed and built our own Christmas wreath from real foliage, including fir, eucalyptus, berries, pine cones, dried oranges and more! It was a lovely, social and creative way to end the day.
