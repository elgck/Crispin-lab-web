---
title: "Research"
lede: "We develop computational methods that use data collected in routine cancer care, including pathology slides, CT and other imaging, genomics and clinical records, to detect cancer earlier, predict how it will behave and personalise treatment."
---

The Crispin Lab works across machine learning, medical imaging, computational pathology, genomics and oncology. We collaborate closely with oncologists, radiologists, surgeons and pathologists at Cambridge University Hospitals and elsewhere, so that our models address clinically relevant questions and can be tested in practice.

Our research covers many scales, from individual cells on a pathology slide to patients followed through treatment. It is organised around the themes below, which share data, methods and people.
