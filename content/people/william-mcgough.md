Dr William McGough completed his PhD in the Crispin Lab (Department of Oncology, University of Cambridge; Robinson College), where he developed deep-learning methods for the automated early detection of kidney cancer in routine non-contrast CT. His thesis, *Deep Learning for the Automated Early Detection of Kidney Cancer in Non-Contrast Computed Tomography*, was submitted in 2025.
