Dr Shangqi Gao is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Early Cancer Institute, University of Cambridge. Before joining the Crispin Lab, he was a Postdoctoral Research Assistant at the Big Data Institute, University of Oxford.

His research focuses on probabilistic modelling, medical image analysis and Bayesian deep learning. In the Crispin Lab, he works on explainable integration of kidney cancer radiology and pathology.

## Education

- **PhD in Statistics**, School of Data Science, Fudan University
