Dr Zeyu Gao is a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Oncology at the University of Cambridge, working in the Crispin Lab and affiliated with the CRUK Cambridge Centre and the Early Cancer Institute. He develops machine learning methods for whole-slide pathology, including multimodal foundation models, spatial omics and clinical prediction from histopathology. His current research is supported by GE HealthCare, and he is a Postdoctoral Fellow of Trinity College, Cambridge (2024–2026).

He led the development of [SMMILe](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-025-01060-8) for spatial quantification in digital pathology (*Nature Cancer*, 2025) and [ALPaCA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76372-z) for slide-level question answering with large language models (*Nature Communications*, 2026).
