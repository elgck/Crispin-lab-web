Hania is a PhD student in the Crispin Lab at the Early Cancer Institute. Her research applies machine learning and large language models to clinical, imaging and molecular data. She worked on the integrative analysis of treatment response in the NAXIVA kidney cancer trial (*Nature Communications*, 2025) and has developed methods that use language models to reconstruct patients' clinical histories in chronic liver disease (*npj Precision Oncology*, 2026).
