Dr Inês Prata Machado is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Cambridge, affiliated with the Early Cancer Institute, the CRUK Cambridge Centre and St John's College. She is also Research Theme Lead for Medical AI at Cambridge's Brain Cancer Virtual Institute. Inês develops artificial intelligence and deep learning techniques to analyse and interpret biomedical data, with a particular focus on medical imaging and computing technology to improve the diagnosis and stratification of patients with cancer. Her research is funded by the Mark Foundation for Integrated Cancer Medicine. She collaborates with GE HealthCare to translate AI models into clinical practice.

Inês lectures in computational biomedicine, machine learning and translational research at Cambridge, including on the MPhil in Data-Intensive Science. She supervises undergraduate, master's and PhD students from the University of Cambridge, University College London, the Netherlands Cancer Institute, Utrecht University, the University of Lisbon and the University of Porto. She is co-founder of the [Cambridge MedAI Lecture Series](https://www.integratedcancermedicine.org/research/cambridge-medai-seminar-series/) and the [Cambridge AI Club for Biomedicine](https://www.milner.cam.ac.uk/ai-club/).

See her [Cambridge Festival Speaker Spotlight](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/cambridge-festival-speaker-spotlight-ines-prata-machado) for more on her brain cancer research.
